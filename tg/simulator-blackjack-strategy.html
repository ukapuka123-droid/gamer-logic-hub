How the trainer works

The recommendation follows a basic strategy table for European late-hole-card rules. The trainer distinguishes hard hands, soft ace hands and pairs.

S17/H17 and DAS settings affect borderline decisions. At a real table, the exact chart also depends on surrender, hole-card checks and other rules.

Cards and points are virtual. The simulator accepts no wagers and promises no winnings.
