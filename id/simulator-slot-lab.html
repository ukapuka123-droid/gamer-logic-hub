What the lab demonstrates

RTP is the theoretical share of all stakes returned over a very large sample. Volatility describes the payout pattern: frequent small results or rare large ones.

Why do results differ?

Every spin is independent. Random deviation can be large over 100 or 1,000 trials. A larger sample generally moves the observed RTP closer to the target, but never guarantees an exact match.
