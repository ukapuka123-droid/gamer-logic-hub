European vs American roulette

A European wheel has 37 pockets: numbers 1–36 and one zero. An American wheel has 38 because it adds 00. With the same payouts, the second zero raises the house edge from 2.70% to 5.26% on most bets.

Results are independent: previous numbers do not change the probability of the next spin.
