How Blackjack works

Number cards count at face value; jacks, queens and kings count as 10; an ace counts as 11 or 1 when 11 would cause a bust. After your decision, the dealer reveals the hidden card and draws to 17.

Chips are provided free of charge, cannot be bought or withdrawn, and have no real monetary value. The simulator accepts no real bets and awards no prizes.
