How Mines works

At the start of a round, mines are randomly placed under 25 cells. Every crystal found reduces the safe cells remaining and raises the multiplier. Collect after any successful pick or continue for a higher multiplier.

The multiplier is based on the probability of opening safe cells in sequence, with a small 3% educational margin. With 3 mines, for example, the first safe-pick chance is 22 out of 25, or 88%.

Chips are provided free of charge, cannot be bought or withdrawn, and have no real monetary value. The simulator accepts no real bets and awards no prizes.
