Slot review: how the game and bonus round work

In a nutshell: Sweet Bonanza uses a 6×5 grid with no paylines to memorise. Matching fruit and candy can land anywhere, winning symbols tumble away, and four lollipops unlock 10 free spins. It looks like a sweet shop discovered fireworks, but the maths underneath is very serious.

↻Regular spins: forget paylines

A win starts when at least eight matching symbols appear anywhere. They do not need to touch or form a neat line. The more matching symbols on screen, the larger the paytable award.

Winners disappear and fresh symbols drop in. The grid is checked again, so one paid spin can produce several Tumbles. Sometimes it stops after one modest hit; sometimes the fruit puts on a small avalanche.

%RTP and volatility

The original game is commonly listed with a theoretical 96.48% RTP. That is a very long-run model, not a promise to return 96.48 out of every 100 credits tonight.

Volatility is high, so quiet stretches and sharp spikes are both part of the design. Some operators use lower-RTP configurations; check the information panel in the exact version you open.

Worth remembering: judging the game by one lucky round is a bit like reviewing a whole year of weather after one sunny Tuesday.

🍭Triggering the bonus

The Scatter is the large pink-and-white lollipop. Landing four, five or six starts 10 free spins. During the feature, three or more lollipops add another five spins.

×Free spins and multiplier bombs

Free spins introduce bombs from x2 to x100. A bomb only matters when the current Tumble contains a win. Multiple values are added: x25 + x50 + x8 becomes x83, not multiplication mayhem.

The original release advertised a maximum of up to 21,175× the stake. A huge bomb without a winning group is still just a very photogenic ball.

×2Double Chance: more chance, higher cost

Double Chance roughly doubles the chance of triggering the feature by making lollipops more likely, while raising each spin's cost by 25%. A 100-credit spin becomes 125 credits.

It does not double every win. It changes bonus frequency and the price of each attempt; RNG still decides the result.

✓The verdict

Eight symbols pay, four lollipops open the bonus, and bombs boost winning Tumbles. Behind the cheerful colours sits a high-volatility model that can stay quiet for a while.

Treat it as random entertainment, not a puzzle that becomes beatable after a few spins. The candy has multipliers; your budget should have a limit.
