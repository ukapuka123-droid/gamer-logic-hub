How to read a slot: the complete glossary from RTP to Max Win

Wilds, scatters, 10,000 ways and a x500 badge can make one info screen look like a boss fight. I have translated the lot into normal English, with the hype dialled down and the useful bits left in.

Published: September 8, 2026 · ~18 min read

RTPRNGWILDSCATTERMAX WINBONUS BUY
Quick answer: RTP is a long-run average, volatility describes the swings, RNG selects the spin result, Wilds help complete combinations, and Scatters usually pay anywhere or trigger features. None of these terms predicts the next spin.

My order is simple: total bet, pay rules, RTP, volatility and win cap first; flashy feature names second. Otherwise the marketing has already sent it while the maths is still tying its shoes.

01

Screen basics

What you see before the first spin.

Slot#slot

A digital reel game whose rounds follow a fixed mathematical model.

In plain English: The game itself, not an income plan.

Spin#spin

One complete round from pressing the button to settling the payout.

In plain English: One click is one separate maths event.

Bet#bet

The total amount risked on one spin and usually the base for x wins.

In plain English: Read total bet, not the prettiest small number.

Reels#reels

Vertical columns of symbols shown spinning during a round.

In plain English: Reels are UI, not physical wheels.

Rows#rows

Horizontal symbol positions; 5×3 means five reels by three rows.

In plain English: Grid size alone says nothing about value.

Paytable#paytable

The rules screen listing combinations, multipliers, features and sometimes RTP.

In plain English: If rules are buried, my eyebrow goes up.
02

Slot maths

The numbers that matter more than a “system”.

RNG#rng

The random number generator selects the outcome; the reel animation reveals it.

In plain English: The RNG has no idea you are “due.”

RTP#rtp

Theoretical return across enormous turnover, not cashback for your session.

In plain English: A 96% label does not owe your $100 session $96.

House edge#house-edge

The model’s long-run share; roughly 100% minus RTP for a slot.

In plain English: That gap is the model’s long-run edge.

Volatility#volatility

The shape of risk: frequent smaller wins or rarer, chunky hits.

In plain English: Higher volatility means bigger swings, not better value.

Variance#variance

A statistical measure of result spread around the average.

In plain English: Wider spread makes short runs feel savage.

Hit frequency#hit-frequency

The share of spins recording any counted payout, including sub-bet wins.

In plain English: Loads of tiny hits can still leave you down.
03

How wins form

Lines, ways and clusters speak different payout languages.

Payline#payline

A fixed path where matching symbols must land.

Ways to win#ways

Matching symbols land on adjacent reels without following one fixed line.

Cluster Pays#cluster

Touching matching symbols form a paying group.

Scatter Pays#scatter-pays

Designated scatters pay wherever qualifying symbols land.

Cascades / Tumble#cascade

Winning symbols disappear and replacements can create another win in one spin.

Megaways#megaways

A variable-reel system where symbol counts and total ways can change on every spin.

04

Special symbols

What substitutes, triggers or transforms.

Wild#wild

Substitutes for regular symbols, usually excluding Scatter and Bonus icons.

Sticky Wild#sticky-wild

A Wild that remains locked for several spins or a full feature.

Expanding Wild#expanding-wild

A Wild that stretches across a reel or row.

Multiplier Wild#multiplier-wild

A Wild that substitutes and also multiplies the completed win.

Scatter#scatter

A special symbol that pays anywhere or triggers a feature.

Mystery Symbol#mystery

Transforms into one selected symbol type before wins are settled.

05

Bonus features

Free spins, respins and the rest of the feature stack.

Free Spins#free-spins

A sequence of spins charged at no extra stake, often with enhanced rules.

Respin#respin

A repeat spin of all or part of the grid.

Retrigger#retrigger

Reactivates a live feature, usually adding more free spins.

Bonus Buy#bonus-buy

Paid instant entry to a feature for a multiple of the base bet.

Ante Bet#ante-bet

An increased stake that boosts feature-trigger probability, not guaranteed profit.

Multiplier#multiplier

A x2, x10 or x500 factor applied to a win or accumulated total.

06

Limits and play modes

Where convenience turns into turnover.

Hold and Win#hold-win

Symbols lock while empty positions respin; new hits reset the counter.

Jackpot#jackpot

A fixed or growing top prize; progressive pools rise with turnover.

Max Win#max-win

The advertised maximum payout, commonly measured in total-bet multiples.

Win Cap#win-cap

A mandatory payout ceiling that can end the round once reached.

Autoplay#autoplay

A queued series of spins; convenient, but it speeds up turnover.

Turbo Spin#turbo

A faster animation of the same maths, not a hotter game mode.

07

Fairness checks

What can be audited—and what remains random.

Demo Mode#demo

Virtual-credit play for learning the interface without real money.

Bankroll#bankroll

A session budget; I treat it as a spending cap, not chase ammo.

Expected Value#expected-value

The average mathematical outcome over time; negative when RTP is below 100%.

Near Miss#near-miss

An almost-win visual that grabs attention but adds no future probability.

Provably Fair#provably-fair

A method for checking a round after server-seed disclosure.

Nonce#nonce

A round counter combined with seeds in a provably fair calculation.

How I read a slot info panel in one minute

No tea-leaf reading and no hunt for a “charged” game.

1. Total betCheck the real cost of one action.
2. RTPFind the percentage for this exact build and mode.
3. RiskCompare volatility, hit rate and max win.
4. RulesSee what Wilds, Scatters and features actually do.

Four terms that get weaponised by hype

“High RTP means it pays now”No. It is a huge-sample average, not a payout calendar.
“This slot is hot”A heater describes the past; it does not charge the next RNG result.
“It hits often, so it is profitable”Hit rate can include wins smaller than the stake.
“Max Win is my session target”It is an extremely rare ceiling, not a typical outcome.
My take: a useful glossary removes magic instead of adding it. If a term clarifies cost, probability or a limit, keep it. If it is being used to promise an easy banger, switch on the sceptic mode.

FAQ

Which matters more, RTP or volatility?

RTP describes average return; volatility describes the route. You need both to understand risk.

How is a Wild different from a Scatter?

A Wild normally substitutes in combinations; a Scatter pays anywhere or triggers a feature.

What does a x1,000 win mean?

The payout is one thousand total bets unless the rules specify another calculation base.

Does stake size change the odds?

It normally scales the payout without changing probability; any exception should appear in the rules.

Can I predict the next spin?

No. Previous results do not reveal the next independent RNG outcome.
