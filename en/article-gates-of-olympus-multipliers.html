Gates of Olympus — How Multipliers Work

~7 min read

Bonus 425% + 250FS

In short: the original Gates of Olympus has no standalone x5000 multiplier. A multiplier symbol ranges from x2 to x500; x5000 is the cap on the total payout relative to the bet. Reaching it requires a winning symbol combination, multiplier values and the maximum-win cap to interact.

↻What Tumble does

The 6×5 grid pays when at least 8 matching paying symbols appear anywhere. Winning symbols disappear, new ones fall into the gaps, and another win can occur within the same paid spin. A tumble is a continuation of one game cycle, not evidence that the game has become “due” to pay.

×When a multiplier applies

A winged multiplier can land in the base game or free spins, but it applies only when that tumble contains a win. Multiple active values are added, not multiplied: x10 plus x25 produces x35, not x250. During free spins, landed values feed a running total multiplier, making long tumble sequences capable of sharp payout spikes.

⚡Why volatility is high

A meaningful share of potential return is concentrated in uncommon combinations of bonus play, repeated tumbles and wins that coincide with multipliers. This produces many small or zero outcomes and occasional peaks. High volatility describes dispersion; it does not improve the next spin’s chance.

?What is the real probability of the maximum?

It cannot be derived honestly from RTP, volatility or the multiplier list. A calculation requires the hidden symbol weights, multiplier distribution, tumble probabilities and payout-stop rules—the PAR sheet or full RNG probability model. Pragmatic Play publicly explains the mechanics and limits but not those weights.

Correct conclusion: the maximum is mathematically possible, but its exact probability is not available in public data. A specific “1 in N” figure without a certification report or PAR sheet is an estimate, not a verified fact.
