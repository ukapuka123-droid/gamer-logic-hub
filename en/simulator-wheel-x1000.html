How Wheel x1000 works

Each spin selects an outcome from a fixed distribution: 0× — 78.9%; 1.5× — 8%; 1.7× — 4%; 2× — 3%; 3× — 2%; 4× — 4.05%; 1000× — 0.05%. Together, this gives a 97% theoretical return.

The rare ×1000 sector appears once per 2,000 independent spins on average. This is a long-run average, not a promise that it will appear after a certain number of spins.
