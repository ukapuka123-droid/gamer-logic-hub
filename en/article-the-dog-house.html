The Dog House — a review of Pragmatic’s most classic slot!

Published: September 1, 2026

~7 min read

An old-school kennel that still has plenty of bite

Quick take: The Dog House is peak classic Pragmatic Play: a 5×3 grid, 20 paylines, goofy dogs and Wild kennels carrying multipliers. I think the game still works because it keeps things dead simple. Hit spin and you instantly know who’s a good boy—and who just chewed through the bankroll.

While playing, I noticed there’s no bonus-buy button in this original version. That sounds old-fashioned now, but my session didn’t feel dead: the paw Scatters kept teasing the reels and the free-spin round landed naturally. That’s my session talking, not a promise from the RNG; sometimes the third paw arrives, sometimes the game suddenly forgets how “fetch” works.

5×3Classic reels, zero faff

Pragmatic Play’s official page confirms the familiar setup: five reels, three rows and 20 paylines. Card ranks, bones, collars and the canine cast are readable at a glance. The whole thing is bright, cartoony and cheeky. That Rottweiler looks like he knows the next result, but he’s absolutely not sharing the tip.

×3Wild kennels and multipliers

The star mechanic is the kennel Wild. It can appear on reels two, three and four with a ×2 or ×3 multiplier. When multiple Wilds help the same payline, their multipliers add up. In the base game they don’t stick around, so a spicy setup can vanish fast—one little victory lap, then the kennel rolls off-screen.

★Free spins: where the kennels get sticky

Three Bonus symbols trigger the free-spin feature. My screenshot shows 11 spins left because the first one was already underway. Here’s the good stuff: every multiplier Wild that lands stays put for the rest of the feature. An early kennel can keep working through many spins, so the round builds a proper story instead of resetting every few seconds. For once, a dog moving into the yard and refusing to leave is excellent news.

☺What I liked—and what creaks a bit

I liked the honest presentation. There’s no maze of modes, boosters and “super mega plus” buttons. The game teaches itself visually, while sticky Wilds give free spins an easy-to-read bit of tension: each new kennel genuinely changes the remaining round.

The downside is obvious too. The base game feels simple next to modern feature monsters, and without Bonus Buy there’s no express lane to the main event. Still, that’s part of the charm. The Dog House is an old dog with a limited trick list, but loads of personality.

✓My verdict

I think The Dog House earned its classic status. It’s bright, instantly readable and doesn’t hide the fun behind ten settings screens. Free spins are the main attraction: sticky multiplier kennels can turn every later spin into a meaningful sweat. It isn’t Pragmatic Play’s fanciest slot, but it’s one you recognise from the very first woof.
