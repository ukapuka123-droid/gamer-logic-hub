How Crazy Chicken works

The road has seven independent steps. The Forward button models each decision: the chicken either reaches the next position safely or meets a vehicle and ends the round.

On Easy, danger is less likely at each step and multipliers grow more slowly. Harder levels have more risk, so a safe crossing raises the potential result faster. You can collect after any successful step.

Chips are provided free of charge, cannot be bought or withdrawn, and have no real monetary value. The simulator accepts no real bets and awards no prizes.
